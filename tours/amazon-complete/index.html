The Full Amazon
Jungle, River & Wilderness Camp

Five days in the heart of the rainforest: the Meeting of the Waters, Victoria Regia water lilies, jungle survival skills, a night caiman safari, a traditional caboclo family, and an unforgettable night camping deep in the forest.

Venture beyond traditional tourism on a five-day expedition into the heart of the Amazon Rainforest. This immersive journey combines wildlife encounters, jungle exploration, authentic cultural experiences, river expeditions, and an unforgettable overnight camp deep in the rainforest, offering a true connection with one of the world's most extraordinary ecosystems.

Three nights are spent at the Amazon Jungle Lodge on the Paraná do Mamori, in private rooms with air conditioning, private bathroom and full board. The fourth night is spent camping in the rainforest itself.

Five days in the Amazon

1

Day 1

Into the Amazon

Your adventure begins with hotel pick-up in Manaus at 8:30 AM.

Travel to Ceasa Port before boarding a speedboat to the legendary Meeting of the Waters, where the Rio Negro and Solimões Rivers flow side by side without mixing. Continue to Vila do Careiro before driving along BR-319, stopping to admire the impressive Victoria Regia giant water lilies.

Arrive at the Paraná do Mamori River and continue by regional motorized canoe to the Amazon Jungle Lodge, arriving around 12:30 PM.

After lunch, explore the surrounding rainforest by canoe in search of monkeys, sloths, macaws, toucans, and countless other Amazonian species.

Enjoy a breathtaking Amazon sunset before dinner and your first overnight stay at the lodge.

Meeting of the Waters Victoria Regia Lilies Canoe Exploration
2

Day 2

Jungle Survival & Night Safari

Wake before sunrise for a birdwatching excursion as the rainforest comes alive.

Following breakfast, enjoy a guided jungle hike led by a native guide, learning about medicinal plants, wildlife, biodiversity, and traditional jungle survival techniques.

After lunch, experience traditional piranha fishing before returning to the lodge.

Following dinner, head into the rainforest for a thrilling night safari searching for caimans and other nocturnal wildlife.

Overnight at the lodge.

Sunrise Birdwatching Jungle Trek & Survival Piranha Fishing Night Safari
3

Day 3

Amazon Culture & Flooded Forests

Visit a traditional caboclo family and discover their customs, lifestyle, cassava cultivation, and deep connection with the rainforest.

After lunch, enjoy a peaceful canoe excursion through the Amazon's remarkable waterways.

During the high-water season (approximately March to September), navigate through enchanting flooded forests (igapós) and narrow rainforest channels (igarapés), providing exceptional opportunities for wildlife photography and birdwatching.

The evening is yours to relax by the river, admire the stars and moonlight, or simply enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of the rainforest.

Overnight at the lodge.

Caboclo Family Igapós & Igarapés (seasonal) Starry Night
4

Day 4

Wilderness Camp

After breakfast, continue exploring remote areas of the rainforest by hiking and canoe.

In the afternoon, venture deeper into the jungle to establish a traditional rainforest campsite.

Learn practical survival techniques while helping prepare camp, cook over an open fire, and experience spear fishing demonstrations (seasonal).

Spend an unforgettable night surrounded by the sounds of the Amazon rainforest beneath a sky full of stars.

Jungle Camp Survival Skills Spear Fishing (seasonal)
5

Day 5

Final Amazon Experience

After breakfast, enjoy one final activity with your guide, tailored to weather conditions and group interests. Depending on the season, you may revisit a favorite location or explore a new area of the rainforest.

Lunch is served either at the lodge or with a local riverside family before beginning the return journey to Manaus.

Estimated arrival is approximately 5:00 PM, with drop-off at your hotel or Manaus International Airport.

Final Excursion Riverside Lunch Hotel or Airport Drop-off
